# First Choice Fashion Designer — Product Showcase

## Files
- `index.html` — page structure and content
- `style.css` — responsive luxury black / warm ivory / gold design
- `script.js` — 30 product entries, carousel, WhatsApp links and jQuery animations
- `assets/images/Heroimg.jpg` — your uploaded hero image
- `assets/images/cloth1.jpg` through `cloth10.jpg` — add your 10 clothing photos here

## Set up
1. Upload the folder to your hosting or open `index.html` in a browser.
2. Keep the hero image named exactly `Heroimg.jpg`.
3. Add your 10 clothing images in `assets/images/` and name them `cloth1.jpg`, `cloth2.jpg`, ... `cloth10.jpg`.
4. Open `script.js` and replace `2348012345678` with your WhatsApp number (Nigeria format, digits only; e.g. `23480XXXXXXXX`).
5. Edit the prices in the `products` array logic in `script.js`. The starter prices are sample prices, not confirmed prices.

## Product images and the 30-item catalogue
The catalogue displays Cloth 1 to Cloth 30. The ten image files are reused in sequence for the 30 entries, so you only need to supply ten clothing photos to start. To use 30 unique photos later, change `imageNumber` in `script.js` to `n` and add `cloth11.jpg` through `cloth30.jpg`.

## Notes
- jQuery is loaded from the official jQuery CDN, so an internet connection is needed for the jQuery animation.
- Google Fonts are loaded online; the site still works with fallback fonts if unavailable.
- Replace sample product prices and WhatsApp number before publishing.
