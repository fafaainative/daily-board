Communication habits


  • Say the result first: “I help you get more customers.” Then explain how.
  • One idea per sentence. Cut every word that does not help the listener.
  • Ask one question, then stop talking and listen.
  • Handle “it’s expensive” with “What would one new customer a week be worth to you?”
  • End with a next step and a time: “Can I send the sample today at 4pm?”
  • Practise out loud for 5 minutes daily. Record yourself.
